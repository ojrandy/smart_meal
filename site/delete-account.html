Delete your Smart Meal account

Smart Meal is made by OJ Tech. You can delete your account and all the data linked to it at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Smart Meal and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If you last signed in more than 10 minutes ago, the app asks you to sign in again first, so nobody else can delete your account from an unlocked phone.

Your account and data are deleted straight away.

By email

If you can't use the app, email ojongrandy01@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Smart Meal account, from the email address you use to sign in. We confirm by reply and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, sign-in details and profile (name, photo).
  • Your setup answers, including allergies, diet and health goals.
  • Your favorites, meal plans and the history of recipes you viewed, saved, cooked or rated.
  • The notification tokens of your devices.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. The recipe catalog stays, because it is not your data. Backups made by our database provider are overwritten as part of its normal backup rotation.
